# CLAUDE.md

Notes for AI-assisted work on this repo. Read README.md first.

## Rules

- **Where things live:** read `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` first, and `docs/HOW-TO.md` for the usual changes. One idea per file: pages and their parts in `apps/web/src/features/<area>/`, each service as `config.js`, `models/`, `routes/`, `lib/`.
- **Logo:** the Pirento wordmark and mark are the files in `apps/web/public/brand/`; render them with `PirentoLogo` / `PirentoMark` (`components/layout/PirentoLogo.jsx`, `tone="light"` on dark panels). Never redraw the logo in CSS or text. Product sites keep their own name and colour.
- **Colours:** from the brand guidelines (`docs/BRAND_GUIDELINES.md`, Appendix A): register in `packages/shared/src/data/brand.js`, tokens in `apps/web/src/styles/index.css`. Product sites get their accent and dark via `useSiteTheme` (`apps/web/src/lib/site.js`). Keep `brand.test.js` passing.
- **Design is fixed.** Layout, type and motion follow `docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`. Colours come only from the CSS tokens (`bg-paper`, `text-ink`, `bg-accent` …); never put a hex value in a component. Accent stays under ~5% of a screen. Never two dark (`inverse`) sections in a row, except contact → footer.
- **No invented facts.** Numbers, names, quotes and claims come from data files only. Stats are counted from the catalog (`apps/web/src/data/site.js`). Unbuilt products are labelled "Expected".
- **Copy lives in data.** Site text in `apps/web/src/data/site.js`; products and prices in `packages/shared/src/data/`. Components only render. The one runtime exception: the announcement banner and paused checkouts, set in admin → Site settings (catalog service).
- **New personal data** in a service: add it to that service's `/internal/forget`, so account deletion removes it.
- **Services stay separate.** One service = one folder in `services/`, its own database (Mongo; the wallet's is PostgreSQL), reached only through the gateway at `/api/<name>`. Add a new one to `SERVICES` in `services/gateway/src/config.js`, the root `dev` script, `docker-compose.yml` and the `Dockerfile`.
- **Shared helpers:** `createApp`, `finish`, `validate(zod)`, `requireAuth`, `requireRole`, `ApiError`/`notFound`/`badInput`/`conflict`, `isId`, `nextSeq`, `encrypt`/`decrypt`, and the Razorpay helpers (`razorpayConfig`, `createRazorpayOrder`, `checkoutSignatureOk`, `webhookSignatureOk`) from `@pirento/shared/server`. Express 5 catches async errors, so routes are plain `async` functions. Responses are always `{data}` or `{error: {code, message}}`.
- **Two areas:** customer pages use `useSignedIn()` and send staff to `/admin`; order and ticket pages, which both sides use, pass `area: 'any'`. Operations and support staff only see and open work assigned to them or not yet taken; finance and superadmins see all of it.
- **Ownership:** paying for or cancelling an order goes through `findOwn` (the customer's own order only); `findAccessible` is for reading and for staff work.
- **Staff roles:** `support`, `finance`, `staff`, `superadmin`. Guard staff routes with `requireRole(...ACCESS.<area>)`; keep `ACCESS` and `ADMIN_SECTIONS` (`apps/web/src/features/admin/adminNav.js`) in step. Every staff change calls `audit(req, action, target)`.
- **Dashboards:** each product's dashboard is its own app: its sidebar lists only its sections (`productDashboards` in `data/site.js`, routes `/<slug>/dashboard/<section>`). The Pirento dashboard is the account centre, not a product launcher: it lists no products, and holds payments (`/payments`), receipts (`/receipts`), orders (`/orders`), subscriptions, wallet, support and account for every product. Sidebars never link to the hub or to Verify. A receipt opens under its product (`/<slug>/dashboard/receipts/<id>`). A customer's order opens under its product too (`/<slug>/dashboard/orders/<id>`), with its messages and documents; `/orders/<id>` stays for staff. Order documents are encrypted with `ORDERS_FILE_KEY`. Every product shows the one wallet (`/<slug>/dashboard/wallet`) and links to the one support desk (`/support?product=<slug>`, themed by that product; tickets keep `product` and `priority`). The desk has tabs (`?tab=tickets|chat|email|call`); live chat and callbacks are tickets too. Admin queues filter by `?product=`; the ticket queue ranks high priority first in the database. The hub routes people into product dashboards rather than repeating their work.
- **App product dashboards** (Intern, Vault, AI, One, Bharat ID): sections in `productDashboards`, words in `appDashboards` (`data/site.js`); each page renders its sections inside `AppFrame` (`features/products/AppFrame.jsx`) and gets `{product, section}` from `ProductDashboardPage`. Capital stays a single waitlist page while Expected. Pirento One and Vault open only once the person's Bharat ID is issued: their services guard with `requireBharatId` (`@pirento/shared/server`, asks identity's `/internal/issued`) and the dashboard shows `BharatIdGate` until then. Vault password health is judged on the server (`/vault/credentials/health`); only verdicts leave.
- **Layouts:** dashboards and admin use `layout: 'app'` in `routes.jsx` (sidebar, no footer); sign-in uses `layout: 'auth'`.
- **Customer notices:** when staff change something a customer must know (order status, a team message, a booking, a ticket reply, a withdrawal decision), call `notify(userId, {brand, label, big, note})` from `@pirento/shared/server`; auth emails it and never fails the caller.
- **Staff search:** queue routes take `?q=` through `searchFilter(q, numberField, textFields)` (a number finds the record, text is escaped).
- **Internal calls:** a service calls another with `callService(url, '/internal/…', body)`; the receiver guards with `requireInternal`. Never expose an `/internal` route without it.
- **Delivery records:** Green (planting site, date, coordinates) and Giftox (courier, tracking) orders carry a `fulfilment` record the team writes through `PATCH /orders/staff/:id/fulfilment`; fields, checks and the customer email line live once in `FULFILMENT` (`packages/shared/src/orders/rules.js`). The customer sees it on their order.
- **Button hover:** `Button` uses a named group (`group/btn`); use named groups in new hover effects so a `group` parent (an order row, a card) does not trigger them.
- **Orders:** status moves only through `STAFF_TRANSITIONS` (`packages/shared/src/orders/rules.js`), with an update conditional on the old status. Intake answers are validated by `validateIntake` on the server. Staff book for a customer through `POST /orders/staff/book` (`ACCESS.booking`): same price and intake checks, the order lands unpaid, no money moves, `bookedBy` records who.
- **Sequences:** order, ticket and Bharat ID numbers come from `nextSeq(name)` in `@pirento/shared/server`. Bharat IDs only through `bharatIdFromSeq`; validate with `isBharatId`.
- **Pirento Intern** (learning service): tracks are the built-in ones (`@pirento/shared/exam`) merged with admin's `Track` rows (`lib/bank.js`: minutes, pass mark, how many questions are drawn, on/off); a track's bank is the built-in questions plus admin's `Question` rows (switched off, never deleted). Each attempt draws its questions at random and keeps the track's minutes and pass mark from when it started. Lessons (`Lesson`, practice answers shown after a check) and `Progress` are made in admin → Intern courses (`ACCESS.learning`). Applying needs the one resume per person (`Resume`, encrypted with `LEARNING_FILE_KEY`). The pipeline lives on `Application`: moves only through `TEAM_MOVES` / `APPLICANT_MOVES` (`lib/pipeline.js`), each conditional on the old status; tasks are sent and reviewed with conditional updates; completing needs every task approved and issues an internship certificate (`kind: 'internship'`, track `internship-<application id>`). The same team routes (`routes/team.js`) serve staff (`/staff/applications`, `ACCESS.openings`) and approved companies (`/employer/applications`, only their own openings). Companies ask (`Employer`), staff approve in admin → Intern companies; a company's openings wait closed until staff approve them, editing their words sends them back to review, and pausing a company closes them. The applicant sees it all in Intern → Internships; a company works in Intern → Company.
- **Intern programme** (learning service): 3, 6, 9 and 12-month places (`PROGRAM_MONTHS`, mark weights and grades in `packages/shared/src/data/internProgram.js`); a superadmin sets each price in admin → Intern programme (`Program`, paise; no row, no sale). A place is bought from the wallet (ref `intern-program-<user>-<months>-<day>`), needs an issued Bharat ID, and one runs at a time (partial unique index). Paid work needs `eligibility` (`lib/program.js`): running place, Bharat ID, a passed track exam, a profile. Daily/weekly/monthly `Assignment`s are claimed once per period (unique index, India time) and written by the AI, or from the next lesson when it is off; the AI grades what is sent, else staff do. `Work` (paid work for the Ecosystem, the Foundation or any product): the AI suggests an intern with its reason (a plain rule when it is off), staff assign, review and approve; approval credits the wallet with ref `intern-work-<id>`, and a failed credit stays `paid: false` for "Pay again". Live classes are a time and a Meet/Zoom link; pressing Join while it runs counts attendance. Lesson videos and class recordings go straight from the browser to S3-compatible storage with signed URLs (`lib/video.js`, `VIDEO_S3_*`); only enrolled interns and staff get a play URL. The AI jobs (task, review, match, resume) live in `services/ai/src/lib/intern.js` with their own frozen system prompts and structured output, reached at `/internal/intern/:kind`; `lib/ai.js` returns null when the AI is off so every caller has a fallback. Finishing fixes the mark (`scoreOf`) and issues a `program` certificate when it passes.
- **Exam answers** (`@pirento/shared/exam`) are server-only. The web app must never import that module.
- **Encryption at rest** (Vault files and passwords, and KYC videos recorded on Pirento before Digio) uses `encrypt`/`decrypt` from `@pirento/shared/server` (AES-256-GCM). Never return plaintext outside the routes made for it. Those old KYC videos are deleted once an ID is approved.
- **Bharat ID on Digio:** after the seven steps, the video KYC (`DIGIO_KYC_TEMPLATE`, with DigiLocker) and the Aadhaar eSign of the declaration (`lib/declaration.js`, e-stamped with `DIGIO_ESTAMP_TAG`) run on Digio through `services/identity/src/lib/digio.js` and `lib/verification.js`; the browser opens Digio's web SDK with the request id and token the server returns (`features/identity/digio.js`). Status comes from the signed webhook (`/identity/digio/webhook`, `X-Digio-Checksum`, always answer 200) or a refresh call. Submit needs `kycDone` and `declarationSigned` (`@pirento/shared`); editing a step or a staff rejection voids the signature. Pirento never stores the KYC media or the signed PDF: staff and the applicant stream them from Digio.
- **Bharat ID for Business:** an API for banks, NBFCs, schools, colleges, institutes and websites, in the identity service. Requests only (`POST /identity/business/requests`, public page `/bharat-id/business`, fields in `BUSINESS_REQUEST`); staff approve in admin → Bharat ID for Business (`ACCESS.business`), which calls auth's `/internal/business-account` to make the account and email the user ID, first password and sign-in link (`SITE_URL`; off production the password also comes back as `devPassword`). The business console (`/bharat-id/business/dashboard`, `features/business/`; dark sidebar grouped Console / Build / Account, and a `Console` strip with company, mode and credit on every page) holds company details (`BUSINESS_PROFILE`), live request, API keys and the consent log. Statuses: requested → test → live_review → live (or rejected, suspended, closed). Keys `bid_test_…` / `bid_live_…` are stored as SHA-256 only and shown once; test keys answer from the sandbox (`TEST_BHARAT_IDS`, made-up details), live keys need live status, and going live needs the company details and the owner's own issued Bharat ID. API `/identity/v1`: `GET /ids/:bid` (public facts), `POST /consents` (the holder is emailed and decides in their Bharat ID dashboard → Sharing), `GET /consents/:id` (details only while approved, read fresh, only the asked `CONSENT_FIELDS`). Holder decisions and staff moves are conditional updates.
- **Bharat ID started by staff:** any staffer (`ACCESS.enrol`, admin → New Bharat ID) starts an application for someone not signed in (`POST /identity/staff/enrol`: email, mobile, the terms confirmed; auth's `/internal/account-for` finds or makes the account) and may fill the seven steps with them; reviewers see all of them, other staff only their own. The person finishes the rest (video KYC, eSign, PIN: only they may) from a one-time sign-in link (`/continue`, auth's `/internal/continue-link` → `LoginLink`, SHA-256, 3 days, used once) sent by email and SMS to the mobile in the application. Every unfinished application, self-started or not, gets the same link as a reminder 1, 3 and 7 days after its last change (`lib/reminders.js`; each reminder claimed by one conditional update, so it goes once).
- **Enrolment centres:** reviewers (`ACCESS.identity`) make centres in admin → Enrolment centres (`Centre`: random 6-character code); any staffer sees them with counts and downloads the QR or an A4 poster (PNG, drawn in the browser). The QR opens `/bharat-id/start?centre=<code>` on the person's phone: sign in or make an account there, then `POST /identity/start` opens the application and records the first centre (`Identity.centre`). A closed centre's QR stops starting applications.
- **Bharat ID PIN and checks:** the PIN is a step of the application (after signing, before review; submit needs it) and can be changed later in the dashboard → PIN with the current one; the approval email says how to use it and never contains it. A holder's 6-digit PIN (`pinProblem`, scrypt in `services/identity/src/lib/pin.js`; 5 wrong tries in a row lock it 15 minutes, counted atomically) and signs in to Pirento with Bharat ID + PIN + date of birth (`POST /auth/otp/bharat-id/pin` → identity's `/internal/pin-check`). Businesses check a holder with `POST /identity/v1/auth/sessions` (methods and levels in `AUTH_METHODS`; each business may use only the methods and ask only the consent fields staff allow in admin, both empty until set). The person does the check on Pirento's page `/bharat-id/authorize/:id?t=…` (the token is stored as SHA-256; 5 tries, 10 minutes; returnUrl must be on the business's website), so a business never sees a PIN or a code. Codes for checks use auth's `/internal/codes/send|check` under their own key. Aadhaar OTP and face run on DigiStudio templates (`DIGIO_AADHAAR_TEMPLATE`, `DIGIO_FACE_TEMPLATE`, `lib/providers.js`; pass only when Digio approves; Setu is the backup slot, not wired yet); app QR, fingerprint and iris are Expected. Live calls are prepaid from the owner's wallet at the superadmin's price list (`lib/billing.js`, ref `bid:<kind>:<id>`, 402 when short), and every call writes the access log (`AccessLog`, never the details) that staff and the holder (Sharing) can read. Pirento never stores Aadhaar numbers, biometrics or KYC media.
- **Redis** (`REDIS_URL`): gateway rate limits are shared across servers, and hot reads use `cached(key, ttl, load)` with `forgetOnWrite(schema, key)` on the model (`@pirento/shared/server`). Without it the cache is per process. A Redis outage never fails a request.
- **Search** (`OPENSEARCH_URL`): Intern lessons and open roles are indexed by their models' save hooks (`services/learning/src/lib/search.js`); `/learning/search` checks each hit against Mongo (bulk closes skip the hooks) and falls back to an escaped regex without OpenSearch. The command palette shows these hits under the local ones.
- **Many copies behind a load balancer:** every service is stateless; `start()` sets a bounded Mongo pool (`MONGO_POOL`), keep-alive above the balancer's idle timeout, and drains on SIGTERM. `/health` answers 503 while the service's database is unreachable (`createApp(name, {ready})` adds other stores). Built web assets are cached for a year (hashed names); `index.html` never is. Background jobs must claim work with conditional updates, since every copy runs them.
- **Sessions:** never put the access token in storage. `authFetch` / `api` in `apps/web/src/lib/api/client.js` attach it and refresh once on a 401; use them for every call, binary ones included.
- **Claude calls** live only in `services/ai`. Model `claude-opus-5-5`; keep `SYSTEM` in `services/ai/src/lib/prompt.js` byte-stable (no dates or ids) so the prompt cache keeps hitting. The team's knowledge notes (`Knowledge`, admin → Pirento AI) go in a second cached system block; where a chat was started (`?product`) goes last as a mid-conversation system message. The assistant's one tool `my_account` (`lib/tools.js`) reads the signed-in person's own account through each service's `/internal/assistant` (wallet, orders, identity, learning, support: short summaries only, never another person's); add a new area there and in `config.areas`. Daily allowance: the free one from `Settings` or a running paid `Plan` bought from the wallet (ref `ai-plan-<user>-<plan>-<day>`), taken with one conditional upsert. One-shot structured jobs (`lib/jobs.js`: Intern tasks and reviews, support reply drafts) are reached at `/internal/intern/:kind` or the staff draft route. Every product dashboard shows Ask Pirento AI (`features/ai/AskAi.jsx`).
- **Effects:** wrap `useEffect` bodies in braces. An arrow that returns a value (e.g. `scrollIntoView`, which returns a Promise in new browsers) crashes React on unmount.
- **Layout check:** `html` has `overflow-x: clip`, so `scrollWidth` never shows overflow. Check element edges against the viewport instead.
- **Prices on screen:** read through `useService` / `useServicesFor` (`apps/web/src/lib/prices.jsx`) so admin edits show; the orders service reads the live service from the catalog service.
- **Catalogue edits (admin → Services & prices):** the catalog service keeps one `ServiceEdit` row per slug (price, off sale, `questions`, `afterQuestions`, or a whole `custom` service made in admin), merged by `applyEdit` / `editedService` / `editedServicesFor` in `packages/shared/src/catalog/services.js`; browser and server use the same functions. A service made in admin has the slug `<product>-<name>`, so pages can tell its product. New services and questions: superadmin only (`ACCESS.catalog`). Legal's category list comes from `useLegalAreas()` (built-in areas first, then new ones like Packages).
- **Questions:** before payment = phone (+ gift address) + the service's own set or the product's defaults (`serviceQuestions`); validated by `validateIntake(service, input)`. Orders keep `asked` (labels) and `afterQuestions` at creation, so later edits never change a placed order; show them with `askedQuestions(order)`. After payment the customer answers on their order (`PUT /orders/:id/details`, while paid or in progress); `needsDetails(order)` drives the dashboard's "Waiting on you". Answers are personal data and are unset by `/internal/forget`.
- **Contact form:** `ContactForm` sits in `ContactCta` on every site and posts to `POST /support/enquiries` without sign-in (honeypot field `website`, own gateway limit `RATE_LIMIT_ENQUIRY`). Staff read and mark them in admin → Enquiries (`ACCESS.support`). Account deletion sends the email to support's forget, which deletes that email's enquiries.
- **Money:** stored in paise (integers) in the wallet service, which keeps its data in PostgreSQL (`services/wallet/src/lib/db.js`, tables made on start): every move is one SQL transaction (`lib/move.js`) whose balance update only matches when the result stays ≥ 0 (and a CHECK forbids below 0), with the ledger's unique `ref` making a retried move a no-op. `scripts/migrate-from-mongo.mjs` copies an old Mongo wallet once. Prices are read on the server; never trust an amount from the browser. Wallet top-ups go through Razorpay (`services/wallet/src/routes/topup.routes.js`): the server fixes the amount, and the browser's verify and the webhook both credit through `move` with ref `topup-<razorpayOrderId>`, so the money lands once. Test money (`POST /wallet/topup`) is refused in production. Cancelling a paid order refunds it the way it came: wallet by ref, card/UPI through Razorpay's refunds API with idempotency key `refund-<paymentId>-<tries>` (a `refund.failed` webhook bumps `tries` so "Retry refund" sends a fresh one); a card payment that lands on a settled order is refunded by itself. A discount code counts as used only when its order is paid (`markPaid`). One open withdrawal per person is a unique index (on `open`), so save the request before debiting. Withdrawals sent through RazorpayX (`lib/payouts.js`) are claimed `requested → processing` in one conditional update, use the withdrawal id as the idempotency key, and settle from the signed webhook or "check again" (`lib/withdrawals.js`): a reversed payout credits back once by ref. Pirento One commission: the rate comes from the partner's plan (`PartnerPlan` in the orders service, made by a superadmin in admin → Partners: price 0 is the free plan, a paid plan is bought from the wallet for `months` with ref `partner-plan-<user>-<plan>-<day>` and keeps its rate until it ends, `lib/plans.js`); a referred order's share is fixed on it when it completes (same conditional update) and credited to the partner's wallet with ref `commission-<orderId>`; a failed credit stays owed and finance sends it from admin → Partners. Any count with a limit (coupon uses, AI messages, vault bytes, one open request) is taken with one conditional update or a unique index, never read-then-write.
- **Motion:** pages call `usePageMotion(ref)`. Mark elements with `data-reveal`, hero extras with `data-hero-fade`, heading lines with `<Heading data-lines>`. All of it is skipped under reduced motion.
- **New page:** add one line to `apps/web/src/app/routes.jsx` above the `/:slug` product routes, call `usePageMeta`, keep one `<h1>`.
- **Bharat ID form:** steps and rules live once in `packages/shared/src/identity/form.js`; the browser and the identity service both validate with `validateSection`. Never store a full Aadhaar or account number.
- Shortcuts are marked `// shortcut: <limit>, <when to upgrade>`. Grep for them before launch.

- **Comments:** each file starts with one line saying what it is for; comment the why, not the what.
- **Tests:** run the e2e stack with `SUPERADMIN_EMAILS=boss@example.com SUPERADMIN_STAFF_ID=root.admin SUPERADMIN_PASSWORD=change-me-now-123 OTP_RESEND_SECONDS=0`, plus the Digio stand-in (`node scripts/digio-standin.mjs`) and `DIGIO_URL=http://127.0.0.1:4090 DIGIO_CLIENT_ID=test DIGIO_CLIENT_SECRET=test DIGIO_WEBHOOK_SECRET=test-digio-webhook DIGIO_ESTAMP_TAG=TEST_STAMP` (as in `.github/workflows/ci.yml`).
- **Test logins:** admin = `SUPERADMIN_STAFF_ID` + `SUPERADMIN_PASSWORD` at `/admin/login`; customer = `TEST_USER_ID` + `TEST_USER_PASSWORD` (user ID + password at `/login`), both seeded at boot by `services/auth/src/lib/seed.js`. Leave `TEST_USER_*` empty on the live site.
- **Accounts:** mobile, user ID and password rules live once in `packages/shared/src/account/rules.js` (browser and auth service both use them). A new account always records `termsAcceptedAt` and `TERMS_VERSION`; change `TERMS_VERSION` when `/terms` changes.
- **Sign-in:** codes (email and SMS) go through `lib/otp.js` in the auth service; the staff door answers every failure with the same message. Never return `passwordHash` (it is `select: false` and stripped in `toJSON`).

## Hosting

Production is AWS (Mumbai) from `deploy/aws` (Terraform: CloudFront + WAF, ALB, ECS Fargate auto-scaling, MongoDB Atlas, Aurora PostgreSQL behind RDS Proxy, ElastiCache Redis, OpenSearch, S3, AWS Backup). `docker-compose.yml` is the one-server setup. Capacity, limits and restores: `docs/SCALING.md`.

## Check before pushing

`npm test`, `npm run build`, `npm run e2e` (with the stack running), `npx prettier --check .`, and a look at 360px and 1440px in light and dark.
