# **1. Executive brand standard**

**Pirento is the masterbrand for one connected ecosystem. Its identity
must feel coherent before it feels varied: every product inherits the
Pirento construction logic, Plus Jakarta Sans typography, disciplined
spacing, high contrast, and a shared commitment to clarity and trust.**

## **The non-negotiables**

- Pirento is the masterbrand. Product brands must never visually
  overpower it in ecosystem-level communications.

- The full approved lockup is the default. The icon is reserved for
  compact, already-recognisable contexts.

- Minimum clear space must be protected on all sides. Nothing may
  enter the exclusion zone.

- Preferred digital lockup width is 542 px; compact lockup width is
  379 px; standalone icons begin at 46 px. Below those values, use a
  tested small-format asset.

- Plus Jakarta Sans is the primary typeface. Inter, Arial, and generic
  sans-serif are fallbacks, not substitutes for logo artwork.

- Each brand uses its canonical dark and accent colours. Never sample
  colours from screenshots or use the contradictory labels in older
  source decks.

- Bright accents generally carry dark text; dark fields carry white
  text. Contrast must be measured, not judged by eye.

- Claims must be truthful, evidenced, current, and approved by the
  responsible product and legal owners.

- The registered-trademark symbol may appear only as supplied in
  approved artwork and only after ownership/status is verified.

- No product may imply government, financial, legal, security,
  environmental, or employment guarantees without formal authority and
  evidence.

## **One system, controlled expression**

The system uses a consistent Pirento wordmark construction, a small
corner device above a distinctive letterform, and an assigned product
accent. The masterbrand is restrained; the product brands express
different categories through colour and tone while retaining a common
visual grammar.

# **3. Brand platform**

## **Core idea**

**Pirento connects specialised products through one recognisable,
disciplined ecosystem. The masterbrand creates trust and continuity;
each product adds a focused promise and its own controlled accent.**

## **Working brand pillars**

> **CLARITY** Make complex systems easier to understand. Use plain
> language, visible hierarchy, and one primary action.
>
> **TRUST** Be accurate, transparent, respectful of user data, and clear
> about limitations and responsibilities.
>
> **DESIGN PRECISION** Use controlled assets, measured spacing,
> consistent typography, and deliberate visual hierarchy.
>
> **USEFUL INNOVATION** Technology must solve a real problem. Avoid
> novelty for its own sake and explain practical value.
>
> **CONNECTED GROWTH** Show how products work together without erasing
> their specialised roles.

## **Brand personality**

Pirento should feel modern but not fashionable for its own sake;
ambitious but not inflated; precise but not cold; confident but not
absolute; and connected without appearing monolithic.

## **Audience principle**

Write and design for people first: customers, learners, professionals,
partners, administrators, and communities. Product-specific terminology
may vary, but every touchpoint should explain what it is, why it
matters, what happens next, and where responsibility sits.

> **APPROVAL NOTE** The source books do not contain a formally approved
> mission, vision, tagline, or market positioning statement. The
> platform above is a faithful synthesis of repeated source themes and
> should be ratified by the directors before external use.

## **Naming rules**

- Use the exact canonical name on first reference. Do not substitute
  filename spellings, abbreviations, or informal nicknames.

- Use title case in prose: Pirento Green, Pirento Capital, Pirento
  Black, Pirento Intern, Pirento Legal, Pirento One, Pirento Vault,
  and Pirento AI.

- Use the endorsed form Giftox by Pirento and Bharat ID by Pirento
  when the relationship must be explicit. Do not reverse the
  endorsement.

- The wordmark is artwork; its lowercase styling does not change
  title-case treatment in written copy.

- Use \'Pirento ecosystem\' as a descriptive phrase and \'Pirento
  Ecosystem\' only when it is treated as a formal programme or
  document title.

- Do not create a new \'Pirento + noun\' brand without a naming
  review, domain and trademark screening, product-role definition, and
  approved visual system.

## **Endorsement hierarchy**

In masterbrand communications, lead with Pirento and organise products
beneath it. In a product-owned experience, lead with the product lockup
and identify Pirento as the parent through the approved lockup,
endorsement line, footer, or account framework. In partner
communications, Pirento or the relevant product should appear once, with
equal visual respect and enough separation from partner marks.

> **DO NOT** Do not combine two Pirento product logos into a new hybrid
> lockup. Use a masterbrand-led ecosystem layout or a written
> relationship statement instead.

## **Clear space**

Protect a minimum exclusion zone on every side of the complete artwork.
For the masterbrand and most family lockups, X equals one-half of the
indicated P/p reference height. Bharat ID uses one-half the B height,
and Pirento AI uses one-half the i height. If the artwork file includes
a larger safety box, preserve the larger value.

> **PRACTICAL RULE** When the original construction grid is unavailable,
> measure the reference letter in the approved artwork and preserve at
> least 0.5x on the top, bottom, left, and right. Clear space is not a
> suggested margin; it is a protected zone.

## **Background and colour selection**

- On white or very light backgrounds, use the approved dark/accent
  lockup.

- On dark brand backgrounds, use the approved white/accent or
  full-white lockup.

- On photography, place the logo in a quiet high-contrast area or use
  a solid brand field; do not rely on drop shadows.

- On an accent field, choose the source-approved contrast version and
  measure legibility at the final size.

- When one-colour reproduction is necessary, use the supplied black or
  white monochrome asset; do not convert a full-colour screenshot to
  grayscale.

## **Prohibited alterations**

- Do not stretch, condense, rotate, skew, outline, bevel, animate, or
  add effects.

- Do not move, resize, or replace the corner device, product name,
  endorsement, or trademark symbol.

- Do not type the wordmark with Plus Jakarta Sans or any other font;
  the logo letterforms are fixed artwork.

- Do not recolour individual letters or use unapproved gradients.

- Do not place text, patterns, partner logos, UI controls, or image
  details inside the clear space.

- Do not use low-resolution crops from the source PDFs as final
  production artwork.

## **Colour roles**

- Dark colours: primary text, navigation, dark fields, footer areas,
  and high-trust surfaces.

  illustration emphasis, and product recognition.

  separation.

process approximations derived mathematically and must be converted
through the printer\'s ICC profile and physically proofed for final
production. Metallic, fluorescent, uncoated, fabric, vinyl, and
illuminated applications require supplier-specific matching.

## **Typesetting rules**

- Use sentence case for headings, buttons, navigation, and labels
  unless a campaign art direction is formally approved.

- Use a line length of approximately 45-75 characters for body copy.

- Set body line height to 1.45-1.65 on screen and 1.2-1.35 in print,
  depending on width and size.

- Avoid all-caps paragraphs. Reserve uppercase for short kickers,
  codes, and controlled metadata.

- Do not use underlining for emphasis; reserve it for conventional
  hyperlinks.

- Use tabular numerals for financial, legal, ID, vault, and dashboard
  data where alignment matters.

- Fallback stack: Inter, Arial, sans-serif. A fallback may replace
  body type temporarily, never the logo artwork.

# **9. Imagery, iconography, and layout**

## **Imagery principles**

- Show real people, environments, products, and outcomes whenever
  available; avoid generic stock symbolism.

- Use natural, credible moments with enough negative space for
  hierarchy. Do not place a logo over a visually noisy focal point.

- Represent India and global audiences with dignity, regional breadth,
  varied ages, abilities, occupations, and settings relevant to the
  product.

- Do not use visual stereotypes for finance, law, sustainability,
  internships, national identity, gifting, security, or AI.

- Record consent, model releases, location rights, licences, creator
  credits, expiry, and permitted channels for every asset.

- AI-generated imagery must be labelled internally, reviewed for bias
  and accuracy, and never used to fabricate documentary evidence or
  customer results.

## **Iconography**

Use a single icon family with consistent stroke, corner radius, optical
weight, and bounding box. Product accent colours may identify active or
selected states; neutral icons should remain in the ecosystem dark. Do
not use the product logo icon as a generic functional symbol.

---

Stroke 2 px at 24 px, with optically adjusted terminals

interface

## **Layout system**

- Build on an 8-point spacing system: 4 px only for micro-adjustments;
  8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, and 96 px for standard rhythm.

- Use generous white space and a clear dominant action. Product accent
  should guide attention, not fill every component.

- Align text, cards, tables, and media to a shared grid. Avoid
  ornamental misalignment.

- Use border radius consistently: 8-12 px for product UI, 16-24 px for
  large cards, and circles only for true circular controls or avatars.

- Use elevation sparingly. Prefer borders, spacing, and tonal surfaces
  before large shadows.

# **11. Applications and co-branding**

## **Co-branding rule**

Partner marks must remain separate, proportionate, and visually
balanced. The source books use Y as the minimum distance between marks.
For most Pirento-family lockups Y equals the height of the lowercase o;
Bharat ID uses the B height; Pirento AI uses the i height. Where partner
guidelines demand more space, use the larger requirement.

## **Visual relationship**

- Align logos on an optical centreline, not merely their file bounds.

- Give each mark its own clear space before measuring the inter-logo
  gap.

- Do not place a connector, slash, box, or shared tagline between
  marks unless both brand owners approve the lockup.

- Use equal visual weight for true partnerships; use a clear hierarchy
  for \'powered by\', \'supported by\', initiative, sponsor, or parent
  relationships.

- Never recolour a partner mark to match a Pirento product without
  written permission.

# **12. Accessibility and inclusive design**

Accessibility is a brand requirement, not a production afterthought. A
recognisable identity that cannot be perceived, understood, or operated
consistently is not a successful identity.

## **Inclusive execution**

  images appropriately.

  visible focus, and labelled form controls.

  horizontal scrolling for ordinary reading.

  forms that match the relevant locale and user context.

  journeys such as identity, legal, finance, account, security, and
  application flows.

## **File naming**

Use:
pirento\_\[brand\]\_\[asset\]\_\[orientation\]\_\[colour\]\_\[space\]\_\[version\].\[ext\]

> **EXAMPLE** pirento_green_lockup_horizontal_primary_rgb_v1-0.svg

## **Format rules**

- SVG: default for web and product UI; optimise without converting
  essential shapes or removing metadata needed for accessibility.

- EPS or vector PDF: print and supplier exchange where requested.

- PNG: transparent raster delivery at 1x, 2x, and 3x where needed;
  never upscale a small PNG.

- JPG/WebP/AVIF: photography only; not for transparent logos.

- RGB: screen and digital files. CMYK: printer-managed production
  files after proofing.

- Never deliver source screenshots, contact sheets, or images cropped
  from these guideline PDFs as final artwork.

## **Version control**

Each approved asset must have an owner, approval date, version, checksum
or immutable file identity, status, permitted channels, and replacement
history. Superseded files remain archived but visibly marked DO NOT USE.
External agencies receive a dated package and must return or delete
obsolete assets after an update.

## **Exception process**

1.  Describe the exact rule that cannot be followed and the business or

    > technical reason.

2.  Provide the proposed design, channels, geography, audience,

    > duration, and risk assessment.

3.  Obtain written approval from the relevant decision owners before

    > release.

4.  Record the exception, asset version, start date, expiry date, and

    > rollback owner.

5.  Do not turn a one-off exception into a new standard without updating
    > this manual.

## **Review cadence**

claim review for major launches, and a full annual guideline review.
Trigger an immediate review after a company-name change, ownership
change, trademark decision, regulatory development, major product
repositioning, accessibility incident, or public brand-risk event.
